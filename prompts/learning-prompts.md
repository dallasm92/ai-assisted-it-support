# Learning Prompts (IT Concepts)

## A) Teach + test (progressive difficulty)
Teach me <topic> like I’m studying for <exam or role>.
Then quiz me with 10 questions:
- 6 basic
- 3 intermediate
- 1 scenario-based
Do not show answers until I submit.

## B) Build a mental model + examples
Explain <topic> using:
- a simple mental model
- a real-world analogy
- 3 examples from Windows/Linux networking
- common mistakes and how to spot them

## C) Flashcard generator (high quality)
Create 20 Anki-style flashcards on <topic>.
Rules:
- short prompts
- one fact per card
- include 5 scenario cards
- include common pitfalls
